## 3. Sakana AI Launches Sakana Fugu: An Orchestration Model That Routes Tasks Across a Swappable Pool of Frontier LLMs
- 출처: https://sakana.ai/fugu-release/
- 발행: 2026-06-22
- 본문:
  Sakana Fugu is a new product that delivers a full multi-agent orchestration system as a single foundation model, dynamically orchestrating the world's best models to tackle complex, multi-step tasks, accessible through a single model API.
  Fugu is itself an LLM, trained to call various LLMs in an agent pool, including instances of itself recursively, and the system autonomously manages the entire lifecycle of model selection and verification using learned coordination strategies rather than hand-designed workflows.
  Two tiers are available: Fugu for everyday work with strong performance and low latency, and Fugu Ultra (fugu-ultra-20260615) for complex, high-stakes tasks such as AI research, cybersecurity analysis, and multi-step patent investigations.
  Fugu Ultra pricing: $5 input / $30 output per one million tokens, with a free second month for users who subscribe by July 31, 2026.

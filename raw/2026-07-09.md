## 3. Introducing GPT-Live
- 출처: https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-live/
- 발행: 2026-07-08
- 본문:
  OpenAI launched GPT-Live on Wednesday, a pair of new voice models — GPT-Live-1 and GPT-Live-1 mini — that replace ChatGPT's existing Advanced Voice Mode.
  The new architecture can listen and speak simultaneously, in a full-duplex design meant to feel like a real human conversation.
  The models are rolling out globally across iOS, Android, and ChatGPT.com.
  GPT-Live-1 becomes the default voice model for paid ChatGPT users (Go, Plus, Pro tiers), while GPT-Live-1 mini serves free-tier users.
